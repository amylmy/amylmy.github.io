Highlights

Ph.D. in Photogrammetry and Remote Sensing (Wuhan University, LIESMARS; Remote Sensing ranked 1st globally since 2017) with postdoctoral training in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at HKUST, complemented by an interdisciplinary dual degree in Engineering and Business Administration. Active research spans ubiquitous positioning, embodied navigation, and AI for education, continuously feeding authentic engineering cases into project-based learning. Brings extensive leadership across tech and policy, serving as a PNT specialist drafting HKSAR Lands Department's Pervasive Positioning Standard and a seconded expert to the Guangzhou DRC/AI Industry Office contributing to local AI legislation to bridge technical prototypes with regional policy and industry needs.
